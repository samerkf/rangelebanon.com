Questions

Good to know.

Where do you deliver?

Beirut, Hazmieh, the Metn up to Bikfaya and the coast up to Jounieh, all the same day. More areas follow once the first ones run smoothly.

How much is delivery?

One flat $3.50 per order, however many brands are in it. With Range Pass, delivery is free.

How can I pay?

By card or Whish in the app, or cash at the door in dollars or lira.

What if I want to return something?

Book it in the app within 7 days and a rider collects it from your door for free. Your refund is added to your Range wallet as soon as we check the item.

I run a brand. How do I join?

Fill in the short form in the For brands section and our team will contact you.
